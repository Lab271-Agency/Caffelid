# Caffelid 1.0.0

Keep your Mac awake with the lid closed, with one coffee cup in the menu bar.

## Download

**Recommended: Caffelid.dmg** — open it and drag Caffelid into Applications.
`Caffelid.zip` contains the same app as an alternative download.

**Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 13 or later.** App and DMG are
Developer ID signed and Apple notarized. This is a community-tested release;
every Mac model and macOS version has not been validated.

## Included

- A native menu bar switch with no Dock icon.
- Lid-closed sleep prevention and display-sleep handling.
- Battery limit: 10% by default, with a 5–70% range or Disabled. Applies on battery only.
- CPU/GPU temperature limit: 95 °C by default, with a 75–100 °C range or Disabled.
  Sleep is restored after 10 continuous seconds at or above the limit with the lid closed.
- Optional Launch at Login. Every launch starts disabled.
- Saved preferences and English/Italian system-language localization.
- Integrated support service: one app download, no separate installer.
- No analytics, network requests, or password storage.

On the first activation, allow Caffelid in **System Settings → General → Login
Items & Extensions → Allow in the Background** and approve macOS's request.
Later activations use the approved service without another password prompt.

Turning the switch off or quitting restores sleep. The power adapter's automatic
sleep option and display timers are not changed. With an external display
connected, display management is left to macOS.

## Verify the download

Download `SHA256SUMS` alongside the archives, then run in that directory:

```sh
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS
```

The file lists both DMG and ZIP; download both for the complete check. To check
only the DMG, compare `shasum -a 256 Caffelid.dmg` with its line in `SHA256SUMS`.

## Italiano

Scarica **Caffelid.dmg**, trascina l’app in **Applicazioni** e aprila. Alla prima
attivazione consenti Caffelid tra le attività in background nelle Impostazioni

A ogni apertura parte disabilitata; i limiti salvati restano disponibili.
